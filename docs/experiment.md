# Family Sudoku — Creative Experiment

## Research Question
Which character-placement method is easier and more enjoyable for Family Sudoku players: drag-and-drop or click-to-select?

## Why This Matters
Family Sudoku replaces numbers with nine family characters. The way players place characters on the board is an important part of the experience.

Since the target audience includes all ages, the controls should be easy to understand and use.

## Approach A: Drag-and-Drop
Players select a family character and drag it directly onto an empty Sudoku cell.

Advantages:
- Feels interactive and playful.
- Visually connects the character to the selected cell.

Potential disadvantages:
- May be difficult on certain devices.
- Requires more precise mouse or touch movement.

## Approach B: Click-to-Select
Players click an empty Sudoku cell and then click the family character they want to place.

Advantages:
- Simple controls.
- Does not require dragging.
- May be easier for beginners.

Potential disadvantages:
- Requires two selections.
- May feel less interactive.

## Planned Experiment
1. Create a simple prototype of both approaches.
2. Use the same Sudoku puzzle for both methods.
3. Ask a few people to try each approach, varying the order.
4. Measure the time needed to place nine characters.
5. Record placement mistakes.
6. Ask participants which method they prefer and why.

## Evaluation Criteria
- Ease of use
- Number of placement errors
- Time required to place characters
- Player preference
- Accessibility and device compatibility

## Assumption
Click-to-select may be easier for a wider audience, but this has not been verified.

## Planned Decision
Select the method that performs better based on observations and participant feedback. If the findings are mixed, prioritize accessibility and ease of use.
